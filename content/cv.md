## Research Interests

- **Vehicle-Mounted Laser Doppler Velocimetry:** System modeling, calibration, and autonomous navigation applications of vehicle-mounted laser Doppler velocimeters.
- **Inertial Navigation:** Error modeling, calibration, and error mitigation for strapdown inertial navigation systems of different accuracy grades.
- **Inertial-Based Integrated Navigation:** Deep and tightly coupled integration of inertial navigation systems with GNSS, odometers, laser Doppler velocimeters, and other aiding sensors.
- **Multi-Sensor Information Fusion:** Outlier detection, adaptive filtering, deep learning-assisted navigation, and reliable integrated positioning in challenging environments.
- **Distributed Multi-LDV Systems:** Configuration design, system calibration, and autonomous navigation applications of distributed laser Doppler velocimeter systems for vehicles operating in GNSS-denied environments.

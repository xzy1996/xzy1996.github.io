## Research Interests

- **Vehicle-Mounted Laser Doppler Velocimetry:** Design and application of vehicle-mounted laser Doppler velocimeters for acquiring multi-dimensional velocity and attitude-related motion information.
- **Integrated Navigation Technology:** Deep and tightly coupled integration of SINS with GNSS, odometers, laser Doppler velocimeters, and other aiding sensors.
- **Inertial Navigation Technology:** Error modeling, calibration, and error suppression for strapdown inertial navigation systems at different accuracy levels.
- **Distributed Multi-LDV Systems:** Structural design, calibration, and autonomous navigation applications of distributed laser Doppler velocimeter systems for GNSS-denied vehicles.
